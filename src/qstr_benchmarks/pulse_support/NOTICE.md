# Clifford decomposition provenance

The ordered 24-Clifford decomposition table in pulse_support/model.py is
adapted from C3 qt_utils.py at commit
48b7917eb8d84e1471ff9fb911129c354e752dbf:
https://github.com/q-optimize/c3/blob/48b7917eb8d84e1471ff9fb911129c354e752dbf/c3/utils/qt_utils.py

The C3 project distributes that source under Apache-2.0. Its license is retained
as C3_LICENSE. The representation is changed to zero-based tuples of primitive
indices. The three-level Duffing propagator is this project's implementation.
