# References and implementation provenance

- **ORBIT feedback:** J. Kelly et al., Optimal quantum control using randomized
  benchmarking, Physical Review Letters 112, 240504 (2014).
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.0035
- **Pulse calibration:** K. Pack, S. Machnes, F. K. Wilhelm, Benchmarking
  Optimization Algorithms for Automated Calibration of Quantum Devices.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.08555
- **Gate calibration:** N. Wittler et al., Integrated tool-set for Control,
  Calibration and Characterization of quantum devices applied to superconducting
  qubits, Physical Review Applied 15, 034080 (2021).
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.09866
- **QAOA comparison problem:** J. Larson, M. Menickelly, J. Shi, A Novel Noise-Aware
  Classical Optimizer for Variational Quantum Algorithms.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.10121
- **Ising state transfer:** M. Rossignolo et al., QuOCS: The Quantum Optimal Control
  Suite, Computer Physics Communications 291, 108782 (2023).
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.11144
- **Quadratic interpolation engine:** T. M. Ragonneau and Z. Zhang, COBYQA.
  https://github.com/cobyqa/cobyqa
- **CMA-ES implementation:** pycma. https://github.com/CMA-ES/pycma

Q-STR calls installed numerical libraries. The distributions do not vendor their
source or archived simulation environments. Removing experimental labels from
identifiers preserves scientific attribution.
